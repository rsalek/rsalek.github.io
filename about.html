About

BiotechVision provides independent technical and commercial advice on life science tools and analytical technologies. We help investment, strategy and product teams assess how technologies perform in practice, what drives adoption, and how suppliers compete.

Our expertise covers mass spectrometry, NMR, laboratory automation and scientific software. We also advise on omics studies, including metabolomics and lipidomics, with support for study design, method selection, data quality and interpretation.
