Privacy

This website, https://biotechvision.co.uk/, is a static personal page for BiotechVision, the metabolomics consultancy of Dr Reza Salek. It does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or other third-party trackers, and it does not collect personal data through forms. If you send a message to the contact address published on the site, that correspondence is handled as ordinary email.

The same address is also used as the homepage and privacy-policy URL for a personal Google OAuth application named “Pi backup”. That application is used only by the site owner to back up his own Raspberry Pi to his own Google Drive. It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, which is limited to files the application itself creates. It is not offered to the public, does not collect data from third parties, and is not a consumer product.

Google may show this page when someone (in practice, only the owner) is asked to grant that application access to Google Drive. No other Google APIs are described here, and no visitor accounts are created on this website.
