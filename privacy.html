Privacy

This website, https://biotechvision.co.uk/, is a static site for BiotechVision. It does not use analytics, advertising cookies or other third-party trackers, and it does not collect personal data through forms. Enquiries can be sent to r.salek@biotechvision.co.uk. Messages to that address are handled as ordinary email.

Google data

The same website address is used as the homepage and privacy-policy URL for a personal Google OAuth application named “Pi backup”. That application uses Google Drive with the drive.file scope only, and only for the owner’s own backup files. It does not share or sell data, and nothing is used for advertising. It is not offered to the public and is not a consumer product.

Google may show this page when the owner is asked to grant that application access to Google Drive. No other Google APIs are described here, and no visitor accounts are created on this website.
